# Slogans

Ideas from the brainstorm of #101 (2026-10-06), kept to come back to them. The chosen one is in `docs/charter.md`.

Writing rule: numbers in words, not mixed with digits ("Three clicks", not "3 clicks" after "One job").

## Chosen

**One job. One resume. Three clicks.**

Short and easy to remember: the tailoring (one resume per job) and the speed (three clicks: import, rank, download).

## Other ideas

| Idea | What works | Why not kept (for now) |
|---|---|---|
| Best about you in three clicks | The most human idea: showing the best of the person | Not idiomatic English; "The best of you, in three clicks" is the correct form |
| The best of you, in three clicks. | Human, plus the speed | Says less about tailoring to each offer; good candidate for a subtitle |
| Best resume in three clicks | Clear, direct promise | "Best" is a generic superlative everyone uses |
| Best resume for everyone | Speaks to free and open | Loses the tailoring and the speed |
| Free, Fast, Fine | Alliteration | In English "fine" means "okay, passable": it undersells |
| Free. Fast. Tailored. | Same rhythm without the "fine" problem | Lists qualities, does not say what the app does |
| Three clicks. One tailored resume. | Says the promise plainly | Longer, less punchy |
| Import. Rank. Download. | The three clicks themselves | A how-to more than a promise |

## Possible subtitles

To pair with the slogan on the landing page:
- The best of you, tailored to every job offer. Free and open source.
- Free, open source, made in France.
